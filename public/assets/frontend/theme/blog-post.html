Getting Started with Agares CMS

SM
Sarah Martinez
January 15, 2026 · 8 min read

Welcome to Agares CMS! Whether you're a developer building client websites or a content creator managing your own site, this guide will help you get started quickly and efficiently.

What is Agares CMS?

Agares CMS is a modern, flexible content management system designed for the needs of today's web. It combines the ease of use of traditional CMS platforms with the power and flexibility developers need to build custom solutions.

Key Features Overview

Before we dive into the setup process, let's review what makes Agares CMS special. The platform includes a visual page editor, comprehensive blog system, analytics dashboard, unlimited custom fields, and powerful user management tools.

Installation & Setup

Getting started with Agares CMS is straightforward. You can choose between our cloud-hosted solution or self-host the platform on your own infrastructure.

Recommended: For most users, we recommend starting with our cloud-hosted solution to get up and running in minutes.

Creating Your First Page

Once you're logged in, navigate to the Pages section in your dashboard. Click "Create New Page" and you'll be taken to the visual editor where you can start building your content immediately.

Publishing Content

When you're ready to publish, simply click the "Publish" button in the top right corner. Your page will go live instantly, and you can share it with your audience.

Next Steps

Now that you've created your first page, explore the other features of Agares CMS. Set up your blog, configure analytics, customize your site's appearance, and invite team members to collaborate.
