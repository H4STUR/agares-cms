Orders

How do I track my order? +
Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking number and a link to track your package in real-time. You can also track your order by logging into your account and visiting the Order History page. Tracking updates are typically available within 24 hours of shipment.
Can I change or cancel my order after placing it? +
You can modify or cancel your order within 1 hour of placing it. After that, orders are processed for shipping and cannot be changed. To cancel or modify, contact our support team immediately via live chat or email at support@nexara.com with your order number.
What do I do if I received the wrong item? +
We're sorry for the inconvenience! Please contact us within 30 days of delivery. Take a photo of the item you received and email it to support@nexara.com along with your order number. We'll arrange for the correct item to be sent and provide a prepaid label for the return of the wrong item — completely free of charge.

Shipping & Delivery

How long does shipping take? +
  • Standard Shipping: 5–7 business days (free on orders $99+)
  • Express Shipping: 2–3 business days ($9.99)
  • Overnight: Next business day ($24.99)

All orders placed before 2pm EST on business days are processed the same day.

Do you ship internationally? +
Yes! We ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by destination. You can see the exact shipping cost at checkout after entering your delivery address. Please note that customs fees and import duties may apply in some countries and are the buyer's responsibility.
What happens if my package is lost or damaged? +
All orders are insured against loss and damage. If your package arrives damaged or is lost in transit, contact us immediately at support@nexara.com. We'll work with the carrier to file a claim and either send a replacement or issue a full refund within 5 business days.

Returns & Refunds

What is your return policy? +
We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy. Items must be in original condition (unused, with all original packaging and tags). To start a return, log into your account, go to Order History, and click "Return Item." You'll receive a prepaid shipping label via email within 24 hours.
When will I receive my refund? +
Once we receive your return and inspect the item (usually within 2 business days), we'll process your refund. The refund will appear on your original payment method within 3–5 business days. You'll receive an email confirmation when the refund is initiated.

Payments & Pricing

What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Shop Pay. All transactions are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption. We never store your full card details on our servers.
Do you offer buy-now-pay-later options? +
Yes! We partner with Klarna and Afterpay to offer flexible instalment plans. You can split your purchase into 4 interest-free payments. Simply select Klarna or Afterpay at checkout. Approval is subject to eligibility.
How do I use a coupon or promo code? +
During checkout, you'll find a "Coupon Code" field in your order summary. Enter your code and click "Apply" — the discount will be deducted from your total automatically. Only one coupon code can be applied per order. Codes are case-insensitive.

Account & Privacy

How do I reset my password? +
Click "Forgot password?" on the Sign In page. Enter your email address and we'll send a password reset link within a few minutes. The link expires after 24 hours. If you don't see the email, check your spam folder.
How is my data used and protected? +
We take your privacy seriously. Your data is encrypted, never sold to third parties, and only used to process orders and improve your shopping experience. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting support. Full details in our Privacy Policy.
